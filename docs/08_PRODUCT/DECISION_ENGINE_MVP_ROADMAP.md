# HYB Decision Engine MVP

> Version: 1.0
> Status: Active

---

# Vision

HYB is not designed to become an ROI calculator.

HYB is designed to become an AI-powered Commerce Decision Engine.

The long-term objective is to answer one question.

> Should I invest my money into this product?

---

# Decision Architecture

Opportunity

│

├── Economics

├── Competition

├── Demand

├── External Market Signals

├── Confidence

└── Recommendation

Every recommendation should consider multiple independent decision dimensions.

---

# Economics

Economics evaluates profitability.

Examples

- ROI
- Profit
- Verified Economics
- Actual Economics
- Variance

---

# Competition

Competition evaluates market saturation.

Examples

- Competitor Count
- Rocket Sellers
- Price Distribution
- Brand Concentration

---

# Demand

Demand evaluates customer interest.

Examples

- Coupang Popularity
- Search Volume
- Growth Rate
- Seasonality

---

# External Market Signals

External services provide additional market context.

Examples

- ItemScout Screenshot
- Human Uploaded Reports

External signals

MUST NOT

- replace Recommendation
- calculate ROI
- overwrite HYB decisions

They exist only to improve context during the MVP stage.

---

# Explainable Recommendation

HYB should explain every recommendation.

Example

BUY

Reason

✓ High ROI

✓ Low Competition

✓ Strong Demand

✓ External Market Signal agrees
